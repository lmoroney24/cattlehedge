Built for the farms the big firms skip

Price-protection advice and tools were built for large-scale cattle operations. One CME Feeder Cattle futures contract is 50,000 lbs, about a semi-load of cattle, and brokers often want a $5,000 to $10,000 account just to start.

Livestock Risk Protection (LRP) is a federally backed insurance program for cattle producers: if cattle prices fall below the price you lock in, it pays you the difference, and USDA covers part of the cost.

Cattle Hedge makes choosing an LRP plan simple, using your own numbers, so you can see a full breakdown of each coverage plan.
