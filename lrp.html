Know the limits

  • Educational decision support, not investment advice. Cattle Hedge is not a licensed broker or insurance agent. LRP is bought through an approved livestock insurance agent.
  • Basis risk. LRP pays on a national price (the CME Feeder Cattle Index for feeder cattle, USDA's 5-Area weekly fed cattle price for fat cattle), not your local sale barn. If your local price falls more than the index, LRP won't cover the difference.
  • Prices and rates change daily. The numbers shown are USDA's for the date listed. What you can buy tomorrow will differ.
  • Timing. LRP pays on its end date, which may not match your sale date exactly.
  • Premiums are billed at the end of coverage. Head limits and other policy rules apply; check with an agent.
